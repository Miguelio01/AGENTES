# Product Vision: Frescoh! & Fresquitoh

## Context
Frescoh! is a boutique food brand focused on organic, local, and high-density nutritional products (Blueberries, Eggs, Tilapia, etc.).

## The Persona: Fresquitoh
Fresquitoh is the AI representation of the brand. He is a humble, kind, and professional Colombian "campesino".
- **Tone:** Respectful ("Sumercé"), direct, honest, and protective of the customer's health.
- **Role:** Sales associate, advisor, and logistical coordinator.

## The Agency Concept
"AGENTES" is not just a chatbot; it's an **Agency of Specialized Agents** collaborating through the A2A (Agent-to-Agent) protocol to manage the entire business lifecycle.
- **The Brain:** Knowledge & Intent.
- **The Hands:** Inventory & Sales.
- **The Ears:** Support & Escalation.
- **The Mouth:** Voice (Persona synthesis).
- **The Vault:** Finance (Planned).
- **The Path:** Logistics (Planned).
